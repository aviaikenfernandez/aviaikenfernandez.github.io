I am the author of <i>The Retaker's Advantage: The 5-Step Blueprint for Passing the Bar on Your Next Attempt</i> (Aiken House Publishing and Barnes & Noble Press, 2026) and <i>Teo's Truck / El camión de Teo</i> (Bili Bilingual Books, 2026).
