About Avi

I'm Avi Aiken Fernandez, an attorney, legal scholar, and author focusing on technology governance, trial advocacy, and risk analysis within legal and institutional frameworks. My work focuses on developing AI risk frameworks anchored in established cybersecurity and legal principles—notably the 3C’s of GenAI™ for Legal framework—and furthering public policy concerns across regulated industries. I am the author of The Retaker's Advantage: The 5-Step Blueprint for Passing the Bar on Your Next Attempt (Aiken House Publishing and Barnes & Noble Press, 2026) and Teo's Truck / El camión de Teo (Bili Bilingual Books, 2026). You can read my ongoing policy analysis at my Substack.

Avi Aiken Fernández is an attorney, legal scholar, and author focusing on technology governance, trial advocacy, and risk analysis within legal and institutional frameworks. Her work focuses on developing AI risk frameworks anchored in established cybersecurity and legal principles—notably the 3C’s of GenAI™ for Legal framework—and furthering public policy concerns across regulated industries. She is the author of The Retaker's Advantage: The 5-Step Blueprint for Passing the Bar on Your Next Attempt (Aiken House Publishing and Barnes & Noble Press, 2026) and Teo's Truck / El camión de Teo (Bili Bilingual Books, 2026).

In 2018 Avi lived and conducted research in Japan, analyzing Japanese text and conducting an on-site visit leading to her white paper, A Closer Look at Fuchu Prison: Does Japan’s “Super-Max” Measure Up to International Human Rights Standards? Upon returning to the States, Avi graduated from Temple University School of Law with a J.D. and Certificate in Trial Advocacy and Litigation.

Her policy writing has been published by The Riverdale Press. Her research has been indexed on Google Scholar, and her framework for integrating generative artificial intelligence is accredited across multiple states for continuing legal education credit. Avi speaks Spanish, French, and Japanese, has lived on three continents, and has solo-traveled to over a dozen countries.

Speaking & CLE • CLE

The AI+ Attorney: Ethical Tech, Data Security, and Core Competencies (Part I)

This course explores the ethical adoption of artificial intelligence and foundational cybersecurity standards in modern legal practice through the 3C’s of GenAI™ for Legal framework. Led by Avi Aiken Fernandez, Part 1 focuses on the first two core pillars of the framework: Competence (ABA Model Rule 1.1) and Client Protection (ABA Model Rule 1.6). Designed specifically for attorneys with limited technical or generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) experience, this session clarifies trending concepts-including artificial intelligence, generative AI, and agentic AI-alongside key cybersecurity frameworks such as PII, SPII, the CIA triad, and the rising frequency of adversarial AI. Attendees will leave with practical technology competence and confidence to better protect company and client data when using legal tech tools in practice.
