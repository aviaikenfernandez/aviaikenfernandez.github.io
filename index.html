I'm Avi Aiken Fernandez, an attorney, legal scholar, and author focusing on technology governance, trial advocacy, and risk analysis within legal and institutional frameworks. My work focuses on developing AI risk frameworks anchored in established cybersecurity and legal principles—notably the 3C’s of GenAI™ for Legal framework—and furthering public policy concerns across regulated industries.

I am the author of The Retaker's Advantage: The 5-Step Blueprint for Passing the Bar on Your Next Attempt (Aiken House Publishing and Barnes & Noble Press, 2026) and Teo's Truck / El camión de Teo (Bili Bilingual Books, 2026). You can read my ongoing policy analysis at my Substack.
