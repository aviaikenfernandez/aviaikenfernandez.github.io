Executive Bios

Short Bio (100 words):

Avi Aiken Fernandez is an attorney, legal scholar, and author admitted to practice in New Mexico, the U.S. District Court for the District of New Mexico, and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Tenth Circuit. She serves on the Federal Bar Association's Government Relations Committee and conducts quasi-judicial merit screenings as an Attorney Panelist on the New Mexico Medical Review Commission. A former investigative prosecutor in the Bronx County District Attorney's Office, she hosts the podcast Common Ground Policy with Avi and delivers CLE instruction on legal ethics and emerging technology, including generative AI.
