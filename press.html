Executive Biography

Short Bio:

Avi Aiken Fernandez is an attorney, legal scholar, and author working at the intersection of law, emerging technology, and public policy. She hosts the podcast Common Ground Policy with Avi and delivers Continuing Legal Education instruction on legal ethics and emerging technology. Fernandez is an active member of the New Mexico State Bar. She is a graduate of Temple Law School and earned a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science with Honors from Spelman College.
