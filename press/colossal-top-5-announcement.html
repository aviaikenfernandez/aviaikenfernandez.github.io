Avi Aiken Fernandez Advances to Top 5 in Colossal's Top Host Competition Featuring Ryan Seacrest and iHeartRadio

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE  |  November 6, 2026
Campaign Beneficiary Notice: Colossal’s Top Host competition operates as a fundraising campaign supporting the Ryan Seacrest Foundation (via Action Initiative Team). The foundation builds Seacrest Studios—interactive broadcast media centers—in children’s hospitals nationwide. Tax-deductible donations made on Fernandez’s behalf count directly as votes for Fernandez while advancing the foundation’s mission to entertain and inspire pediatric patients.

New York, NY — Nov. 6, 2026 — Avi Aiken Fernandez has officially advanced to the Top 5 in Colossal's Top Host competition, presented in partnership with media icon Ryan Seacrest and iHeartRadio. The nationwide search recognizes standout community-based audio talent who demonstrate an authentic connection with audiences and extraordinary potential in the entertainment industry.

Known for engaging presence, unscripted chemistry, and dedication to "holding space" for meaningful dialogue, Fernandez has captivated voters across the country. Her platform highlights authentic storytelling, community-first dialogue, and advocating on behalf of others through her Common Ground Policy with Avi initiative.

"Reaching the Top 5 is both an incredible honor and a testament to the power of community support," said Fernandez. "This platform isn't just about winning a title; it's about holding space at the table for voices that deserve to be heard and building opportunities for future talent. If awarded the grand prize, I am committed to paying it forward by investing directly into local mentorship programs that help community-members find their voice and share it with the world."

As the competition enters its critical final phase, supporters and listeners can cast their FREE daily votes via their Facebook profile and mobile phone verification at https://tophost.org/2026/avi-aiken-fernandez. Tax-deductible charitable donations made on Fernandez's profile double as additional votes while directly benefiting the Ryan Seacrest Foundation.

ABOUT AVI AIKEN FERNANDEZ

Avi Aiken Fernandez is an attorney, author, and host of Common Ground Policy with Avi, a podcast and digital publication exploring policy, legal ethics, and civic engagement. She is the author of The Retaker's Advantage: The 5-Step Blueprint for Passing the Bar on Your Next Attempt (Barnes & Noble Press, 2026) and the bilingual children's book Teo's Truck (El camión de Teo) (Bili Bilingual Books, 2026). Fernandez currently serves as a leadership coach working with local New York City college students, and is active in the legal community as a Continuing Legal Education instructor and bar examination coach. For more information on her work visit her official website at https://aviaikenfernandez.github.io or connect with her on Facebook at https://www.facebook.com/AviAikenFernandez for community updates.
