Avi Aiken Fernandez Advances to Top 5 in Colossal's National Top Host Competition Featuring Ryan Seacrest and iHeartRadio

Official Press Announcement  |  November 6, 2026

NEW YORK, NY — November 6, 2026 — Attorney, policy strategist, and host Avi Aiken Fernandez has officially advanced as a Top 5 Finalist in Colossal's nationwide Top Host search competition, presented in partnership with Ryan Seacrest and iHeartRadio benefiting the Ryan Seacrest Foundation.

The nationwide talent search aims to identify promising broadcasting, hosting, and media voices while driving awareness and fundraising support for pediatric patient programs across the United States through the Ryan Seacrest Foundation's Seacrest Studios.

Aiken Fernandez's advancement to the Top 5 round follows months of public voting, commentary, and platform growth centered on ethical leadership, legal literacy, and non-partisan public discussion. As host of the podcast Common Ground Policy with Avi and author of The Retaker's Advantage, she brings an executive perspective rooted in investigative advocacy, public policy, and institutional governance.

"Advancing to the Top 5 in this national effort is both a privilege and a powerful responsibility. Partnering with a cause that brings entertainment, education, and light to pediatric hospital patients directly reflects the principle of service that guides my work in law and policy."

The final phase of the national competition will culminate in selecting the winner, who will receive featured broadcast opportunities and mentorship support. Further details regarding ongoing voting rounds and updates are maintained across official channels.

About Avi Aiken Fernandez

Avi Aiken Fernandez is an attorney, policy strategist, and author admitted to practice in New Mexico, the U.S. District Court for the District of New Mexico, and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Tenth Circuit. She serves on the Federal Bar Association Government Relations Committee and as an Attorney Panelist on the New Mexico Medical Review Commission. She hosts Common Ground Policy with Avi and lectures on legal technology, ethics, and trial practice.
